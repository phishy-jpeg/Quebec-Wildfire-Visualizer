    About this site

    Wildfires are becoming increasingly common due to forest mismanagement and climate change. While it is very easy to find online graphs on the increase in wildfires, resources to visualize this spatially is limited. This site aims to be a tool for people interested in conservation, to be able to visualize the effects of wildfires over the years.
