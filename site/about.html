About this site

As smoke days in the summer become commonplace in Quebec, this site was created out of general curiosity about the increase in wildfires over the years. While historic wildfire data does exist, and it is very easy to find graphs online showing the increase in wildfires over the years, resources to visualize this change are limited. This site aims to be a tool for anybody who has been affected by wildfires and people generally interested in conservation to be able to learn more about wildfires in Quebec.
